**Real definition:** a dataset for evaluating an LLM’s proficiency in summarizing text. XL-Sum provides entries in many languages; each contains an article from the BBC and a summary of the article. [Google’s glossary: XL-Sum (xlsum) ↗](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/glossary#xl-sum-xlsum)
